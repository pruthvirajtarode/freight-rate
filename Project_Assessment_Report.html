1. Dataset & Data Pipeline Architecture

3. Model Interpretability & SHAP Feature Drivers

To ensure transparency, global SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) values were calculated. The primary decision drivers for freight rates include:

Top Feature Contributions:

    Distance (miles): Strongest positive driver. Rates scale non-linearly over long haul lanes.
    Cargo Weight (lbs): Higher equipment weight increases rate per mile.
    Rate per Mile (Engineered): Interaction term providing clear baseline expectations.
    Market Capacity Index: Captures spot market tight/loose capacity supply dynamics.

4. December 2025 Daily Forecast Insights

The model was applied to the 31 daily input records for December 2025. Key seasonal dynamics captured:

  • Pre-Holiday Surge (Dec 18–22): Peak rate spike driven by holiday shipping volume constraints.
  • Christmas Dip (Dec 24–25): Temporary rate decline due to market operational pauses.
  • Year-End Adjustment (Dec 29–31): Stabilization as annual contracts reset.
