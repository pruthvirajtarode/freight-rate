Interpretation Guide

  • Red Dots (High Feature Value)

    Higher values for this feature push the predicted freight rate higher. For example, high distance pushes the prediction up.

  • Blue Dots (Low Feature Value)

    Lower feature values push the rate lower. For instance, low cargo weights decrease the predicted rate.

  • Horizontal Spread (SHAP value)

    Dots to the right of the center line indicate a positive impact on rate; dots to the left indicate a negative impact.
